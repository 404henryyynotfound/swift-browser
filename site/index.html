01 / Privacy

Privacy isn't a setting.
It's the default.

You shouldn't have to tune a dozen switches to browse quietly. Swift ships with the safeguards on, and keeps them out of your way.

  • ◎

    Trackers & ads, stopped at the door

    Built-in filters block known trackers and intrusive ads before they load — no extension required.

  • ◈

    Fingerprint defense

    Swift reduces the signals a page can use to single you out, from canvas quirks to font enumeration.

  • ∅

    No telemetry, ever

    No usage pings, no crash uploads by default, no account tying your browsing to an identity.

  • ◐

    Private windows that stay private

    Session-only storage, isolated from your regular profile, closed the moment you are done.

02 / Speed

Less overhead.
More momentum.

Speed isn't only about rendering pixels faster. It's about doing less work in the background and starting instantly when you sit down.

01

Tab sleeping

Quiet tabs release memory and CPU after a delay, then wake up the moment you return to them.

02

Turbo mode

One switch tightens resource budgets, defers heavy page work, and keeps the browser light on battery.

03

Instant local new tab

Your new tab page is rendered on-device, so opening it never depends on a remote server.
